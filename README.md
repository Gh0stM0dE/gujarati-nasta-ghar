Welcome to the official repository for **Gujarati Nasta Ghar**, a premium homemade food business located in **Adajan, Surat**. This website is designed to provide customers with a seamless digital experience, from viewing our daily fresh menu to placing orders via WhatsApp.

## ✨ Key Features

- **🍱 Dynamic Digital Menu:** Real-time menu and pricing powered by **Supabase SQL**, allowing for instant updates without changing the website code.
- **🟢 Live Business Status:** An intelligent "Open/Closed Now" badge that automatically updates based on our kitchen timings (8:00 AM - 8:30 PM).
- **💬 Direct WhatsApp Ordering:** One-tap floating button and hero actions to connect customers directly with our team.
- **⭐ Google Reviews Integration:** Professional testimonial section showing real feedback from our Google Maps listing.
- **📍 Interactive Location:** Embedded Google Maps for easy navigation to our shop in Choksi Wadi, Adajan.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3 (Custom Grid Layouts), Vanilla JavaScript.
- **Backend/Database:** [Supabase](https://supabase.com/) (PostgreSQL) for menu management.
- **Icons & Fonts:** FontAwesome 6.4, Google Fonts (Poppins & Great Vibes).
- **Hosting:** GitHub Pages.
